## Institutions that need permission for YouTube use

- **USHMM** (Photo Archives): many images are "courtesy of" third parties. Their terms require permission for broadcast and online use beyond fair use.
- **Yad Vashem** (Photo Archive): permission and a fee for publication of their files. The Commons scans of the Auschwitz Album are the exception noted at C1.
- **Auschwitz-Birkenau State Museum** (Archive): permission is required for all of their photographs.

I will not use any image from these three without listing it here and getting your OK.
